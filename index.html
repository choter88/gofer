01 / The problem

Your staff isn't refusing AI.

They're just being told to use tools that aren't designed for them.

  • Custom integrationsBuild

Half of it has to be learned, half of it has to be built, and the seat you pay for sits unused.

02 / The fix

So we put it in a form they already know.

No login, no training, and no new habits to learn. They email Gofer like they would an intern.

gofer@yourcompany.com

Gofer understands what you need. It learns as you use it and gets better at its job, but never asks for a promotion.

03 / Why me

I've built gofers my whole career.

Twelve years as an engineer on RF and space hardware. Every job I had, I wrote scripts to take the grunt work off my plate. Gofer only wants to do the grunt work and fits into how people already work.

This doesn't replace your best people. It gives them a smart, personal intern so they can do more of what makes them great.
